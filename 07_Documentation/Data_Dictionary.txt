RIDE ANALYTICS & BUSINESS PERFORMANCE DASHBOARD

Data Dictionary

1. Customers

Purpose: Customer ki information store karta hai, jaise name, gender, age, city aur signup date.
Grain: Har row ek customer ko represent karti hai.

2. Drivers

Purpose: Driver ki information store karta hai, jaise name, gender, age, city, joining date aur rating.
Grain: Har row ek driver ko represent karti hai.

3. Vehicles

Purpose: Vehicle ki information store karta hai, jaise vehicle type, model, year aur associated driver.
Grain: Har row ek vehicle ko represent karti hai.

4. Locations

Purpose: Location ki details store karta hai, jaise city, area aur zone type.
Grain: Har row ek location ko represent karti hai.

5. Rides

Purpose: Ride transactions ki details store karta hai, jaise customer, driver, vehicle, pickup/drop location, date, time, distance, fare aur ride status.
Grain: Har row ek ride ko represent karti hai.

6. Payments

Purpose: Payment ki details store karta hai, jaise payment method, payment status aur amount.
Grain: Har row ek payment record ko represent karti hai.

Project Note

This Data Dictionary explains the purpose and level of detail of each dataset table used in the Ride Analytics project.
