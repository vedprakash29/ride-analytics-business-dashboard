# RIDE ANALYTICS & BUSINESS PERFORMANCE DASHBOARD

## Business Insights & Recommendations

### 1. Project Overview
This document summarizes the key findings from the Ride Analytics project and provides recommendations based on ride, revenue, cancellation, vehicle, customer, driver, and payment analysis.

### 2. Key Business Insights

**Insight 1: Ride Volume and Completion**
- Total rides analyzed: 20,000.
- Completed rides: 16,795.
- Ride completion rate: approximately 83.98%.
- Recommendation: Monitor ride completion trends by date, city, and vehicle type to identify operational improvements.

**Insight 2: Ride Cancellations**
- Total cancelled rides, including customer and driver cancellations: 3,205.
- Cancellation rate: approximately 16.02%.
- Customer cancellations: 1,381.
- Driver cancellations: 1,025.
- No-driver-found rides: 799.
- Recommendation: Investigate cancellation patterns by time, location, distance, and vehicle type. Review driver availability and booking demand, especially where no driver is found.

**Insight 3: Payment Performance**
- Paid revenue: ₹20,54,498.80.
- Failed payment amount: ₹31,318.87.
- Payment success rate: approximately 98.48%.
- Payment failure rate: approximately 1.52%.
- Recommendation: Monitor failed transactions and investigate whether failures are concentrated in particular payment methods or periods.

**Insight 4: Vehicle Performance**
- Sedan generated the highest revenue among vehicle categories in the ride-based analysis, approximately ₹8,22,472.32.
- Auto revenue: approximately ₹6,94,902.26.
- Bike revenue: approximately ₹5,79,053.02.
- SUV revenue: approximately ₹3,90,763.70.
- Recommendation: Compare revenue with ride volume, operating costs, and vehicle availability before making fleet allocation decisions.

**Insight 5: Payment Method**
- UPI was the leading payment method by transaction volume and revenue in the analysis.
- Recommendation: Continue monitoring payment method performance and investigate failed payments to improve the customer payment experience.

**Insight 6: Geographic Performance**
- Delhi was the highest-revenue city in the dashboard analysis.
- Recommendation: Compare demand, ride completion, cancellations, and driver availability across cities to identify opportunities for operational improvement.

### 3. Overall Recommendations
1. Investigate the main causes of customer and driver cancellations.
2. Improve driver availability in areas with no-driver-found bookings.
3. Monitor payment failures and payment success rates over time.
4. Compare vehicle performance using revenue, ride volume, and operational costs.
5. Use dashboard filters to investigate trends by date, city, vehicle type, and ride status.
6. Review KPIs regularly to identify changes in business performance.

### 4. Conclusion
The analysis provides an overview of ride operations, cancellations, vehicle performance, geographic revenue, and payment outcomes. The findings can help guide further investigation and support data-informed business decisions. Recommendations should be validated with additional operational and cost data before implementation.
